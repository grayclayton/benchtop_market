# Benchtop Market — Tiered Metric Access & UX Model

## 1. Executive Overview

To optimize market liquidity while driving institutional SaaS revenue ($399–$799/mo), Benchtop Market implements a two-tier information architecture:

1. **Simple Trader View (Free Public Market)**: Designed for retail and community prediction market participants. Focuses on high-level human-readable outcomes, simple pass/fail safety badges, and clear YES/NO prediction questions.
2. **Investor Pro Terminal ($399/mo Institutional SaaS)**: Designed for angel investors, venture capital partners, family offices, and technical analysts. Provides granular electrochemistry telemetry, 7-gate numerical measurements, cryptographic IPFS verification hashes, and raw data reduction downloads.

## 3. Detailed View Specification

### Tier 1: Simple Trader View (Public)
* **Target Audience**: Retail traders, clean-tech enthusiasts, community members.
* **Goal**: Minimize decision friction and eliminate cognitive overload from dense physical science equations.
* **Key UI Elements**:
  * **Milestone Summary**: *"Will WSEI's 200-cycle bench test meet all lab safety, lithium purity, and extraction efficiency standards?"*
  * **Status Badges**:
    * `✓ All 7 Lab Gates Passed`
    * `✓ Seawater Purity Verified`
    * `✓ Zero Toxic Chlorine (Safe)`
  * **Target Metric Display**: *"100% Pass across 7 Lab Gates"*
  * **Investor Pro Upsell Teaser**: Prominently highlights that raw electrochemistry telemetry is unlocked with Investor Pro.

### Tier 2: Investor Pro Terminal (Paid Subscription)
* **Target Audience**: Hardware & deep-tech investors, technical due diligence partners, family offices.
* **Goal**: Provide institutional-grade verification, founder team intelligence, and direct deal-sourcing access.
* **Key UI Elements**:
  * **Founder & Team Dossier**: Founder bio (`Dr. Clayton Gray`), technical background, team size, and academic/lab pedigree.
  * **Patent & IP Portfolio**: Summary of filed patents (e.g. *WSEI Wave-Synchronized Intercalation Architecture*, *Self-Cleaning Conducting Graphene Skin*).
  * **Open Fundraising Round Data**: Target valuation, open round status ($1.5M Seed Target — $500k Soft Committed).
  * **Direct Investor-to-Founder Messaging Channel**: One-click `[ Message Founder ]` action to initiate direct investor communications.
  * **Gate-Level Telemetry Table**:
    | Gate # | Benchmark Parameter | Measured Value | Target Threshold | Status |
    |---|---|---|---|---|
    | Gate 1 | Li/Na Selectivity ($S_{\text{Li/Na}}$) | $1.77 \times 10^6$ | $\ge 1.0 \times 10^5$ | **PASS** |
    | Gate 2 | Faradaic Efficiency | $99.25\%$ | $\ge 90.0\%$ | **PASS** |
    | Gate 3 | Molar Purity ($\text{Li}^+$) | $94.10\%$ | $\ge 90.0\%$ | **PASS** |
    | Gate 4 | Lithium Enrichment | $14.20\times$ | $\ge 10.0\times$ | **PASS** |
    | Gate 5 | Mn Dissolution Rate | $0.040\,\mu\text{mol/m}^2\text{-cycle}$ | $\le 0.100\,\mu\text{mol/m}^2\text{-cycle}$ | **PASS** |
    | Gate 6 | Oxidant Safety ($\text{Cl}_2/\text{ClO}^-$) | Non-detect ($<0.05\,\text{ppm}$) | Below lab safety limit | **PASS** |
    | Gate 7 | Structural Degradation | Zero delamination | No structural loss | **PASS** |
  * **Cryptographic IPFS Hash**: Immutable proof hash logged to IPFS oracle network.
  * **Data Terminal Export**: One-click raw JSON data inspector modal.

## 4. Economic Rationale & Monetization Value

1. **Higher Trading Volume**: Simplifying trader metrics removes decision paralysis, driving higher trade counts and liquidity (which generates the 0.5% platform protocol fee and 2.5% testing fund escrow).
2. **Defensible SaaS Paywall**: Hiding granular numerical telemetry behind VC Pro gives venture funds a compelling, concrete reason to subscribe to the $399/mo tier rather than relying solely on free public data.
