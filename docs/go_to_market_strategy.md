# Benchtop Market — Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy & Execution Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

Benchtop Market is a prediction market and milestone-funding platform engineered for deep tech startups (clean energy, direct lithium extraction, robotics, biotech, quantum computing). The GTM strategy leverages an **Investor-Only Freemium Engine** ($0 startup listing fee, $399/mo Investor Pro terminal subscription, 2.5% testing fund escrow, 0.5% platform protocol fee) to solve the deep tech "Valley of Death" funding gap.

```
  PHASE 1: PILOT & PROOF-OF-CONCEPT        PHASE 2: SPONSOR & LAB NETWORK         PHASE 3: SCALE & NETWORK EFFECTS
        (Months 1 – 3)                           (Months 4 – 6)                          (Months 7 – 12)
```

### Phase 1: Lighthouse Pilot (Months 1–3)
- **Anchor Campaign**: Use **The Lithium Eel** (WSEI Lithium Extraction) with its real 7-gate pass/fail bench test data as the primary lighthouse case.
- **Paper Trading Competition**: Host a 30-day paper-trading tournament with a $5,000 sponsored cash prize pool for top-ranking technical predictors.
- **Cohort Onboarding**: Onboard 5 high-profile seed-stage startups across DLE, solid-state batteries, robotics, and synthetic biotech.

### Phase 2: Sponsor & Accredited Lab Integration (Months 4–6)
- **Accredited Lab Network**: Partner with 5 independent testing laboratories (SGS, TÜV SÜD, NREL, university core facilities) to establish standard oracle intake protocols.
- **Sponsor Matching Network**: Secure 10 VC and corporate sponsors offering direct 1:1 matching grants ($10k–$25k per campaign).
- **Investor Pro SaaS Launch**: Convert early investor alpha testers into paying $399/mo Investor Pro and $799/mo Institutional API subscribers.

### Phase 3: Scale & Automated Network Effects (Months 7–12)
- **Self-Serve Startup Onboarding**: Open $0 freemium campaign creation for any verified deep tech startup.
- **Target Scale Metrics**: 350 active listing campaigns, 86+ Investor Pro subscribers, $61.7M/mo trading volume, and **$4.36M Platform ARR**.
